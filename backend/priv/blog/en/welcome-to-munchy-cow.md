Audio cleanup tools have a reputation problem. They either do nothing useful, or they process your voice into an uncanny, robotic shell of what it was. We built Munchy Cow because we wanted something better.

## The problem with most audio cleanup

If you've ever recorded a podcast in a less-than-perfect room, you know the drill. There's background hum from the AC, a bit of reverb from bare walls, maybe some street noise sneaking in through the window.

Most tools attack this noise so aggressively that your voice comes out sounding thin and artificial. You trade one problem for another.

## Our approach

Munchy Cow uses a multi-stage processing pipeline that treats your audio with care:

- **Spectral denoising** removes steady-state noise without touching your voice
- **De-reverb** tames room reflections while keeping natural presence
- **Gentle compression** evens out your levels for a polished, professional sound
- **Smart EQ** corrects common frequency problems without making everything sound the same

Each stage is calibrated to do just enough, never too much. The result is audio that sounds clean but still sounds like *you*.
